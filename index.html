DocCap

A private document scanner for Android

DocCap turns your phone camera into a document scanner: it finds the page edges live, corrects the perspective, cleans up the page and saves it as PDF or JPEG in your own folders.

  • Everything is processed on your phone. No account, no ads, no analytics, no tracking.
  • Optional Google Drive copies, end-to-end encrypted on your phone before upload. DocCap can only access the files it created itself (drive.file).
  • Restore your documents on a new phone with your 24-word recovery key.
